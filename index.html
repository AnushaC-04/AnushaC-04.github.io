Featured · ML + LLM · Insurance

Medical Insurance Premium Estimator & Risk Explainer

Predicts an individual's annual medical insurance cost, then uses an LLM to translate the prediction into a plain-language explanation of what's driving the price — built for the underwriter-facing side of premium pricing.

  • Trained a DecisionTreeRegressor on the Medical Cost Personal dataset (1,338 records) to predict annual charges, evaluated with R² and RMSE.
  • Identified smoking status, followed by BMI and age, as the dominant cost drivers via feature importance analysis.
  • Fed model outputs into a LangChain-orchestrated LLM pipeline that generates a readable risk report for policyholders and underwriters.
  • Prototyped in Jupyter; designed for a Streamlit / FastAPI front end for real-time predictions.
